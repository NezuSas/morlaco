Garbage collection schedule in zone 6, Cuenca

EMAC's garbage truck comes by zone 6 of Cuenca on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, 13:00 to 17:00. The light-blue recycling bag goes out on Thursday.

The truck comes by on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, 13:00 to 17:00.

The light-blue bag, with recyclables, only goes out on Thursday.

Black bag: food scraps and anything that isn’t recycled. Light-blue bag: paper, cardboard, plastics, cans and Tetra Pak, clean and dry.

Put the bags out before the collection window starts.

Get a reminder before every collection: subscribe to the zone 6 calendar.
